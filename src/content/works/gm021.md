Ask 'em Y is an experimental music trio consisting of Yu Miao, Ertel Angelina, and Krist Stefan. Their debut album, Ask 'em Y in Germany, features tracks recorded in Berlin and Munich. Six of the tracks are live recordings from the group's concert at EinsteinKultur (also known as MUG). The Munich concert was organized by Offene Ohren e.V. and supported by the City of Munich Department of Arts and Culture and APPLAUS (German Federal Culture Award).

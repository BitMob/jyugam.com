項目來由

maf：肘力不如你寫一下
事由 2023 年尋常一晚，maf 問我，是否對他 2019 年在香港錄下的田野錄音有興趣合作一些新東西？同時期，我們也在考慮是否可以做一些味道相關的出品，加上我還在寫科幻短小說中暈浪。當晚，他還展示一張原應作為專輯封面的照片給我看，其中背景廣告路牌寫著“維多利亞”。這就是這個項目的開始。項目歷時兩年，經歷了很多重的合作。味道由我們對錄音所傳達出的感覺而調制（由 16 度的空調，渡輪汽油，還有中藥房等組成），小說基於錄音寫成，聲音基於小說完成，插畫基於前兩者而成，系一環扣一環的合作方式，最後融合為同一個宇宙。

重聽 2019 年香港的聲音，有點像在挖時間膠囊。在 6 年後的今天看，經歷了一段時間真空。時間是一個力大無窮的巨獸，它可以扭斷很多連結。但希望這個項目可以保護起某種記憶，變成一種物質留存，不要讓它輕易消失。

Project Origin

maf: Can you maybe write something, Jolie?
It started on an ordinary night in 2023, when maf asked me if I was interested in collaborating on something new based on the field recordings he made in Hong Kong in 2019. Meanwhile, we were also considering whether we could do some scent productions, and I was still intrigued by writing sci-fi short stories. That night, he also showed me a photo that was supposed to be the album cover, an old man was waiting for the traffic light with a background advertising sign written “Victoria”. This was the beginning of this project. The project lasted two years and went through a lot of collaborations. The scent was created in the light of the feeling from the recordings, composed of 16-degree air conditioning, ferry gasoline, and Chinese pharmacies, etc.. The story was written based on the field recordings, the sound was completed based on the story, and the illustration was based on the first two. A chain of collaborations that finally merged into the same universe.

Listening to the sounds of Hong Kong in 2019 is a bit like digging up a time capsule. Looking back today, I feel like I have experienced a period of vacuum. Time is a beast that can break many connections. But I hope this project can protect some sort of memories, become a material preservation, and not let them disappear easily.

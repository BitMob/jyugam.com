Social Anhedonia 是 WISEFAKE 在 Jyugam 的實驗三部曲的最新章，也是迄今為止最跳躍且有趣的一章。本作繼承了前作 [strati](https://jyugam.com/work/gm012-·-strati/) 與 [wetloop](https://jyugam.com/work/gm017-·-wetloop/) 的實驗脈絡，在 WISEFAKE 前衛的構架中引入了更多更具親和力的節奏元素。他將凝滯的環境重塑為流動、富有呼吸感的音景，在聲響維度進一步詮釋了他的空間理解。
WISEFAKE 拒絕傳統的田園式表達。他將自己對公共空間的觀察轉化為躁動的聲音書寫，借機探索了對自然的超現代連接。Social Anhedonia 冷峻地折射出我們當下的生存狀態：現代社會與技術粗暴地取締了傳統的生活方式，而我們都已被數字媒體的喧囂永久地改變了感知，人類已經無法像過往一樣與社會、自然或宏大概念產生純粹的連接。

*Social Anhedonia* marks the latest chapter in WISEFAKE’s experimental trilogy with Jyugam, and its most restless and playful entry to date. Following [strati](https://jyugam.com/work/gm012-·-strati/) and [wetloop](https://jyugam.com/work/gm017-·-wetloop/), the album expands his established experimental language with more immediate rhythmic elements, folding accessibility into an otherwise uncompromising framework. Static environments are loosened into fluid, breathing soundscapes, extending his investigation of space through sound.
WISEFAKE avoids any conventional pastoral reading of nature. Instead, observations of public space are translated into unstable, agitated sonic forms, tracing a hypermodern connection to the natural world. *Social Anhedonia* reflects the condition of contemporary life with a cold clarity: modern society and technology have displaced older modes of living, while the constant saturation of digital media has irreversibly reshaped perception. The possibility of an unmediated relationship with society, nature, or larger systems of meaning has become increasingly difficult to recover.

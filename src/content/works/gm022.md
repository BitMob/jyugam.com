這張里程碑式的精選集收錄了 WhiteCrow 音樂生涯中的諸多重要時刻，主要選取了先前在 Jyugam 和 billows in the mind 廠牌發行的作品。它清晰地為我們串聯起 WhiteCrow 藝術歷程中所有多元而豐富的音樂影響，是回顧其風格演變的一份完整聽覺指南。
WhiteCrow 在計劃發起之初，便奠定了其標誌性的聲響風格：將複雜而精巧的節奏結構，與廣闊、流光溢彩的氛圍層疊融為一體。隨著音樂生涯的持續拓展，她巧妙地將流行和後搖滾（Post-Rock）的元素融入到電子框架之中，並始終堅守其內在的實驗精神。通過不斷發展和確立自己獨有的先鋒電子藝術風格，WhiteCrow 鞏固了其在當代中國電子音樂版圖中至關重要的地位。

After a series of critically acclaimed releases, WhiteCrow is back on Jyugam with a stunning compilation of her previously released material (from Jyugam and billows in Your Mind Records).
This compelling collection serves as an auditory bridge, meticulously exploring a vast musical spectrum and connecting the dots of WhiteCrow’s diverse influences throughout her artistic journey.
Venturing between intricately designed beats and vast, shimmering layers of ambient texture, she subtly incorporates elements of pop, post-rock, all without sacrificing the experimental core that defined her early sound. Through the continuous development of her signature style of artful avant-garde electronics, WhiteCrow has further cemented her position as an essential and distinctive voice in the current Chinese electronic music landscape.
